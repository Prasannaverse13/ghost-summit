# Ghost Summit

A daily one-shot vertical climbing escape game for Reddit.

Every day, one mountain — the same for everyone. A ghost rises from the dark below and never stops. Climb, dodge, and shoot your way toward the summit before it catches you. You get **3 climbs a day**, and only your **best score counts**.

## The hook

- **One daily mountain.** A fresh, seeded climb generated each day — everyone plays the exact same course, so scores are truly comparable.
- **3 climbs a day, best score counts.** Three attempts daily; your highest run is the one that lands on the board.
- **Daily leaderboard.** Race the whole community for the day's top height.
- **Daily champion.** Each new day's post crowns yesterday's #1 climber — beat them and take the crown.

## Gameplay

- **Climb to escape.** A relentless ghost rises from below. Keep climbing or get caught.
- **Shoot what hunts you.** Zigzagging bats cross your path — pick them off with your gun or dodge them.
- **Reach the light.** Push through the darkness toward the summit for the highest score you can manage before your run ends.

**Controls** — Desktop: arrow keys to move, up/space to jump, mouse to aim, click (or X/F) to shoot. Mobile: swipe to move and jump, tap to shoot.
